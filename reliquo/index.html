How it works

Three steps. The first one is free.

  1. 1

    A free call

    15 minutes by phone or video. You tell us where calls and messages slip through, and we tell you what could help.

  2. 2

    We set it up

    We build it around the phone and email you already use, and test it with pretend customers before anything goes live.

  3. 3

    A monthly check-in

    Once a month you get a short report on what your helpers did, and we fix or adjust anything that needs it.

What we automate

Small helpers that work in the background while you're on the job.

Missed-call text-back
If you can't pick up, the caller gets a text right away, so they wait for you instead of calling someone else.
Lead logging
Every new call, text and web form goes into one list, so no job gets forgotten.
Follow-ups
Quotes and callbacks get a polite reminder, sent for you at the right time.
Review requests
After a job, your customer gets a short message asking for a Google review.
Email sorting
Your inbox sorts itself into what needs you today and what can wait.

Questions

Do I need to be techy?

No. We do all the setup. After that it runs on its own, and there's nothing new for you to learn or check.

What if it breaks?

Tell us and we'll fix it. We also look everything over before each monthly report.

Who owns my data?

You do. Everything runs on your own accounts, like your Google or Microsoft email. You sign in yourself, we never see your password, and you can switch the connection off any time.

Who is behind Reliquo?

Reliquo is run by Madden, here in Winnipeg. The person on your free call is the same person who sets things up and sends your monthly report.
