What can you help me with?

The small, repeat jobs that fill up your day. Each helper does one of them, the same way, every time.

  • Missed calls

    When you can't pick up, the caller gets a friendly text right away. They know you'll call back, so they don't ring the next business on the list.

  • Unanswered messages

    Questions from your website get a helpful reply with your real hours, prices and service area, even at 9 at night.

  • Follow-ups

    Sent a quote and heard nothing? A polite reminder goes out a few days later, so you don't have to remember.

  • A messy inbox

    Each email is filed in the right folder, and anything you need to act on is flagged at the top.

  • Asking for reviews

    After a job is done, your customer gets a thank-you text with a link to leave a Google review.

How does it work?

  1. We talk it through

    A free, no-pressure chat. You tell me what eats up your time, and I tell you honestly if I can help.

  2. I build it and test it

    I set it up using pretend information first, so nothing touches your real accounts until it works.

  3. We switch it on together

    I walk you through each screen. You sign in yourself, with your own password.

  4. It runs quietly in the background

    You get on with your day. If you ever want it off, it takes one click.

Do I need to be good with computers?

No.

If you can read a text message, you're set. I do the setting up, and I explain everything in plain words. You never have to open anything technical.

Is it safe?

Yes, and I'm careful about it. The most important part: I never see your passwords.

  • You type your own password.Always, even when I'm sitting right next to you.
  • You sign in on the real Google or Microsoft page.Not a page I made. You can check it yourself before you continue.
  • A helper only sees what it needs.One inbox or one folder, never your whole account.
  • You can turn it off anytime.One click, and it's disconnected.
