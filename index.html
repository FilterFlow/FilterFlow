Questions People Usually Ask

You're allowed to be a little skeptical.

Is this actually safe?+

Yes. Anything that connects to one of your accounts uses that company's own official login screen — the same one you'd see anywhere else. I never see or hold your password, and you can disconnect it yourself, anytime, with one click.

Do I need to know anything technical?+

No. You won't need to learn any new software or touch a single setting after we set things up. If anything ever needs your attention, I'll explain it in plain language — no acronyms, no assumed knowledge.

What happens to my data?+

Whatever we set up only ever looks at the one thing it's built for — like a single inbox folder or a call log — never your whole account or unrelated files. Before we connect anything, we'll look at exactly what it's asking permission to do, together.

What if I try it and don't like it?+

Then we turn it off. Nothing here locks you in, and disconnecting anything is always just as easy as connecting it was in the first place.
