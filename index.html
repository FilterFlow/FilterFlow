How it works

Three steps, and you only do the first one.

1

We have a normal conversation.

You tell me what keeps slipping through the cracks. Maybe it's calls you miss while you're on a job, or customers you meant to get back to.

No forms, no tech talk. A phone call or a coffee is fine.

Start that conversation
2

I set it up for you.

For example: when you miss a call, the customer gets a friendly text right away, so they don't call someone else.

3

It quietly does its job.

You keep working the way you always have. There's nothing new to learn or check.

Questions

It's fair to be a little wary.

These are the things people ask me most. If yours isn't here, just ask. No question is too basic.

Is this safe?

Yes. When anything connects to your email or phone, you sign in yourself on that company's own official screen, like Google's or Microsoft's.

I never see your password. You can switch the connection off yourself, any time.

Do I need to know anything about computers or AI?

No. I do all the setting up. After that, there's nothing for you to open, learn or keep an eye on.

If something ever needs your attention, I'll explain it in plain words.

What happens to my information?

Each helper only sees the one thing it's for, like new messages in one inbox. It doesn't see the rest of your account or your files.

Before we connect anything, we'll read the permission screen together, so you know exactly what it can and can't do.

What if I try it and don't like it?

Then we switch it off. There's no contract to get out of, and turning it off is as easy as turning it on was.
